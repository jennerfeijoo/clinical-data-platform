# Pilot offer summary

## Offer

A bounded clinical-data quality and cohort-reproducibility pilot supported by the Clinical Data Platform.

## Recommended initial use case

Hypertension data quality, terminology linkage, and reproducible cohort construction.

## Inputs

- synthetic clinic-shaped data; or
- a clinic-approved de-identified extract processed in a clinic-controlled environment.

## Outputs

- source and field inventory;
- clinic-specific mapping and contracts;
- validation and quarantine report;
- terminology and unit review;
- PostgreSQL load with lineage and durable execution evidence;
- reproducible cohort or quality-analysis output;
- attrition and missingness report;
- limitations, risk register, and expansion recommendation.

## Exclusions

The pilot does not provide patient-specific diagnosis, treatment, triage, automated action, EHR replacement, PHI readiness, regulatory compliance, or production deployment approval.

## Sales statement

> We do not replace the clinic's clinical system. We evaluate whether selected exports can be converted into auditable, validated, terminology-linked, reproducible analytical data for one agreed quality or research question.
